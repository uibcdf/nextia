# Design Boundaries

1. DiscoveryProject state/history ≠ DiscoveryEngine action.
2. Strategy ≠ Protocol; Campaign ≠ Protocol.
3. Artifact ≠ Result ≠ Observation ≠ Evidence.
4. Sabueso SourceAssertion ≠ Nextia Evidence.
5. External knowledge does not automatically become project Evidence.
6. Discovery is graph-shaped, not obligatorily hypothesis-first.
7. Failure, contradiction, rejection, inconclusive results, and supersession are first-class.
8. Human authority and approval gates are first-class.
9. Important persistent objects should be serializable and referencable.
10. Stable identity must be separable from storage location and deployment topology.
