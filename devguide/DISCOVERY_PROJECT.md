# DiscoveryProject

A DiscoveryProject is the persistent, machine-readable scientific state/history of an investigation.

## Conceptual groups

- **Framing:** Focus, Goal, entities/systems and contextual roles.
- **Reasoning:** Questions, Hypotheses, Strategies.
- **Execution context:** Campaigns, Experiments, Runs/references.
- **Outputs:** Artifacts, Results, Observations, Evidence.
- **Design space:** Candidates and collections.
- **Governance:** Decisions, approvals/rejections, provenance, history.

## Graph model

Objects are independently identifiable and connected by references. This supports branching, shared entities, contradictory Evidence, superseding without deletion, and historical reconstruction.

## Integrity

Project history is scientific data. Failed, rejected, contradictory, inconclusive, and superseded states should remain inspectable.
